# egypt-inflation-macro-analysis-
Econometric case study quantifying Exchange Rate Pass-Through (ERPT) vs. domestic demand slack in Egypt (2013–2028). Proves exchange rate shock dominance over output gap for monetary policy design.

1: ASK (Problem Definition & Analytical Framework)Primary Objective: Evaluate the macroeconomic determinants of inflation in the Arab Republic of Egypt (2013–2028) using an Open-Economy New Keynesian Phillips Curve (NKPC) framework to determine whether domestic price spikes are driven by demand-pull dynamics or cost-push exchange rate pass-through (ERPT).
Key Institutional Questions:

What is the statistical significance of exchange rate depreciation ($\Delta \ln \text{FX}$) versus the output gap ($\text{gap}_t$) in explaining consumer price volatility?

How accurately does the empirical regression model ($\pi_t = 5.00 + 0.303 \cdot \pi_{t-1} + 0.131 \cdot \text{gap}_t + 0.387 \cdot \Delta \ln \text{FX}_t$) fit actual headline inflation across major devaluation shocks (2016, 2022–2024)?

Should monetary policy prioritize aggressive domestic interest rate hikes (demand suppression) or foreign exchange liquidity stabilization?
