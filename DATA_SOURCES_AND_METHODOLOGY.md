## 2. Dataset Column Definitions (`Egypt_Macro_Time_Series_2013_2028.csv`)

| Column Name | Unit | Formula / Transformation | Analytical Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `Year` | Calendar / Fiscal Year | Integer (`2013`–`2028`) | Annual observation period. |
| `Data_Type` | Categorical | `Actual` vs. `Projection` | Distinguishes historical institutional records from forward forecasts. |
| `FX_Rate_EGP_USD` | EGP per 1 USD | Period average spot rate | Official Egyptian Pound exchange rate against the US Dollar. |
| `Log_FX_Delta_Pct` | Percentage (%) | $[\ln(\text{FX}_t) - \ln(\text{FX}_{t-1})] \times 100$ | Continuously compounded annual currency depreciation rate. |
| `Real_GDP_Growth_Pct` | Percentage (%) | Annual constant-price growth | Real Gross Domestic Product annual growth rate. |
| `Output_Gap_Pct_Pts` | Percentage Points (pp) | $\text{Real\_GDP\_Growth\_Pct} - 4.50$ | Deviation of actual GDP growth from Egypt's **4.50%** structural potential growth benchmark. |
| `Baseline_CPI_Pct` | Percentage (%) | Annual headline CPI change | Standard baseline inflation trajectory without external fragility stress. |
| `Fragility_CPI_Pct` | Percentage (%) | Annual headline CPI change | Stress-tested inflation series incorporating external geopolitical, maritime, and tariff shocks. |
| `Lag_Fragility_CPI_Pct` | Percentage (%) | $\pi_{t-1}$ | One-year lagged inflation rate measuring backward-looking price inertia. |
| `CPI_Index_2013_Base` | Index (`2013 = 100`) | $(\pi_t / \pi_{2013}) \times 100$ | Normalized inflation intensity index anchored to Base Year 2013. |
| `Modeled_CPI_Pct` | Percentage (%) | $5.00 + 0.303\pi_{t-1} + 0.131\text{gap}_t + 0.387\Delta\ln(\text{FX}_t)$ | Fitted inflation output generated by the Open-Economy Hybrid NKPC OLS regression. |
